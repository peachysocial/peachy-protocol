# Security

Please report a vulnerability privately, by email to security@pchy.app, not in
a public issue or change.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker gains. We
will acknowledge the report, keep you informed while we fix it, and credit you
when the fix is public unless you ask us not to. Our disclosure policy is at
https://pchy.app/security and our bounty terms at
https://pchy.app/security/bounty.

In scope: the MLS core and its bindings in `mls/`, the protocol as specified
in `docs/` and `proto/`, and the behavior of the service at
`https://api.pchy.app` where it differs from the specification.

What the server holds is in [docs/metadata.md](docs/metadata.md).
